# Overview
## Dashboard
This is your Lumina dashboard. Active users, conversion and revenue are always one glance away.
[zoom: Active users]

# Step 1: Create a report
## Open Reports
Open Reports, then choose New report.
[click: New report]
[chip: Reports > New report]

## Configure
Pick a data source, such as product events. Then choose a metric and a date range, and generate the report.
[zoom: product events]
[click: generate]

# Step 2: Read the results
## Results
Your chart appears instantly, with an AI summary of what changed. Lumina also flags anomalies, like this spike in sign-ups.

# Step 3: Share it

Finally, invite your team and send the report.
